## What the AGPL lets you do

This is a plain-words summary, not legal advice. The [license itself](LICENSE) is what counts.

- **Use it.** Anyone may run penrose, for any purpose, at home or at work, at no charge.
- **Read it and change it.** The source is here so that you can.
- **Share it, on one condition.** If you give penrose, or a version you changed, to someone else, on its own or
  inside your own product, you must give them the complete source of your version under the AGPL too.
- **Offer it as a service, on the same condition.** If people use your changed version over a network, you must
  offer them its source.
