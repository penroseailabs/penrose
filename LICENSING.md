A commercial license is available for uses the AGPL does not fit.

## What the AGPL lets you do

This is a plain-words summary, not legal advice. The [license itself](LICENSE) is what counts.

- **Use it.** Anyone may run penrose, for any purpose, at home or at work, at no charge.
- **Read it and change it.** The source is here so that you can.
- **Share it, on one condition.** If you give penrose, or a version you changed, to someone else, on its own or
  inside your own product, you must give them the complete source of your version under the AGPL too.
- **Offer it as a service, on the same condition.** If people use your changed version over a network, you must
  offer them its source.

## Commercial license

If those conditions do not fit what you are building, for example a closed-source product or service based on
penrose, you need a commercial license. It replaces the AGPL's conditions with terms agreed with us, for a fee.

To ask about one, open an issue with the **Commercial license** form. Say who you are and what you want to build;
leave out anything confidential. We will reply there and move the conversation to email.

## Contributions

The licenses above cover the code. They do not give anyone the right to use the name "penrose" or its tile logo
for their own product or service. A changed version that you share must use another name and logo.
